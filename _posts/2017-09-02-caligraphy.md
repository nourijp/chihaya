Fuad Koichi Honda, native to Japan, is one of the top Arabic caligraphy artists in the world.  He is crossing bounderies, bringing Japanese and Islamic culture together, in ways that they can both appreciate, as well as the rest of the world.  
He is a contemporary visual artist who was born in 1946. Several works by him have been sold at auction, including 'Surah Luqman' sold at Christie's Dubai 'Modern & Contemporary Arab, Iranian and Turkish Art Part II' in 2012 for $56,250.
