Iran has a huge food culture and many many different types of foods can be found in Iranian households.  In the video below, ten dishes are featured and discussed.  
According to one of the people featured in the video, most people, including people from the Far East, all the way to people from Europe,  like Iranian foods.  The foods they like include the Iranian kebabs such as "Koubideh" and "Barg". They also love "Dizi" and the various different types of rice.

Watch the video to learn more about different Iranian dishes.
